Fortnite's Guardian Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Fortnite's Guardian collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the bot.

Fortnite's Guardian is an independent Discord application designed to provide moderation, management, utility, and community-management features for Discord servers.

2. Information We May Access

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Fortnite's Guardian may access information provided by Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Nicknames
  • Server membership information
  • User roles and permissions
  • User join dates
  • Server and channel information
  • Message or interaction information required to respond to commands
  • Moderation and bot action information
  • Information provided directly through bot commands

Fortnite's Guardian only accesses information necessary for the features enabled on the server.

3. Information We Store

Depending on how the bot is configured, Fortnite's Guardian may temporarily or persistently store information such as:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Previous nicknames used for restoration or moderation purposes
  • Bot action history
  • Moderation-related records
  • Username or nickname information
  • Server configuration information
  • Information required to process bot commands

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, private messages, or Discord account credentials.

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Fortnite's Guardian may be used to:

  • Provide bot commands and features
  • Manage server members
  • Detect duplicate or suspicious usernames and nicknames
  • Perform moderation and administrative actions
  • Restore or manage nicknames
  • Maintain action history and undo functionality
  • Diagnose bot or server-related issues
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the bot
  • Prevent misuse of the bot

Information is not used for unrelated purposes.

5. Discord Information

Fortnite's Guardian operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform and API systems.

We do not control how Discord itself collects, processes, or stores information. For information about Discord's own privacy practices, please refer to Discord's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally provide collected information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by third-party services only when necessary for the operation of Fortnite's Guardian or its infrastructure.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal requests, or to protect the security and operation of the bot.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the bot's features, maintain functionality, or fulfill legitimate administrative purposes.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or removed from our systems.

Because Fortnite's Guardian operates within Discord servers, some information may remain available through Discord itself even after it is removed from our own systems.

8. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe Fortnite's Guardian has stored information relating to you and you would like it removed, you may contact the bot's administrators with your Discord username and/or Discord user ID.

We may need to verify the request before deleting information associated with an account.

Some information may be retained when necessary for legitimate administrative, security, legal, or operational purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Fortnite's Guardian is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

Users should follow Discord's applicable age requirements and policies when using Discord and Discord applications.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Fortnite's Guardian.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this page will be updated.

Continued use of Fortnite's Guardian after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Contact

For questions, concerns, or data deletion requests relating to Fortnite's Guardian, please contact the bot's administrators through the Discord server where Fortnite's Guardian is being used.

13. Independent Application Disclaimer

Fortnite's Guardian is an independent Discord application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games or Fortnite.
